The Problem

Two things broken about money in Pakistan

Arcistan exists to fix both, on the same rail.

01

Your savings quietly lose value

The Rupee loses 15-25% of its value every year. Money sitting in a bank account or under a mattress is losing purchasing power even when the number on the screen stays the same.

Fix: Convert PKR to USDC in seconds — your savings hold stable dollar value instead of eroding silently.
02

Freelancers have no direct payment gateway

Pakistan has no simple cross-border payment platform built for its freelancers and remote workers. Getting paid by an international client, or sending money home from abroad, means bank wire delays, high cuts, and paperwork.

Fix: Get paid straight into a USDC wallet — flat low fee, no bank in the middle, arrives in seconds, from anywhere in the world.
